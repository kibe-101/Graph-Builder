Based on your project, I actually think your current presentation style is fighting against the subject matter.

Your application is essentially a **network operations dashboard**—it's analytical, mathematical, and engineering-focused. Meanwhile, your presentation theme sounds like something for a startup pitch or an educational technology product. Those aren't necessarily bad aesthetics, but they don't reinforce the message of your dissertation.

## I'd aim for this instead:

> **Modern Research Lab / Cisco Live / IEEE Conference / Microsoft Azure Portal**

Not corporate marketing.

Not playful.

Not dull academic PowerPoint either.

Think **clean engineering presentation**.

---

# Overall Style

### Theme

**Intelligent Network Operations Center**

Imagine a mix of:

* Cisco Live presentations
* Juniper Networks

# Should the app and presentation match?

I wouldn't make them identical.

I'd make them look like they're part of the same product family.

For example:

App

* very dark
* functional
* dashboard

Presentation

* slightly brighter
* more whitespace
* larger typography
* cleaner diagrams

Same

* colours
* fonts
* icons
* diagram style

Different

* density of information

Exactly how Apple's developer documentation resembles Xcode without literally copying it.

---

## The visual direction I'd recommend (9.5/10)

**Theme:** Intelligent Network Operations Center

**Mood:** Modern engineering research, not startup pitch

**Fonts:** Space Grotesk + Inter

**Colors:** Slate + blue + cyan + green + amber

**Graphics:** Network topology, graph theory, heatmaps, RSSI circles, system architecture

**Diagrams:** Large, minimal, with consistent line weights and iconography

**Overall impression:** An examiner should feel they're looking at a polished engineering tool and its accompanying research presentation, with both clearly belonging to the same project while each being optimized for its purpose.
